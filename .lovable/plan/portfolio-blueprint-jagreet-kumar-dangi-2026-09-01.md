## 7. Design and technical direction (for the build phase)

- Palette: Deep Navy & Cyan — `#0A1020` background, `#122040` surfaces, `#22D3EE` accent, `#E6EEF8` text; defined as semantic tokens in `src/styles.css`, no hardcoded color utilities.
- Typography: geometric sans headings paired with a neutral body sans, loaded via `<link>` in `src/routes/__root.tsx`.
- Route: rewrite `src/routes/index.tsx` (replacing the template placeholder); sections as components under `src/components/portfolio/`.
- Assets: your photo and the CV PDF served through Lovable CDN asset pointers in `src/assets/`; Download CV links the hosted PDF.
- SEO: route `head()` with your name in the title, a CV-accurate description, og/twitter tags, single H1, alt text on the photo, and Person JSON-LD built from CV facts only.
- Motion: subtle scroll reveals and hover states; respects reduced-motion.
- No backend needed — the contact section uses `mailto:` and profile links. A database-backed contact form can be added later with Lovable Cloud if you want one.
